AIM:
This project has five Main Aims:
Aim One:
I would like to find a way to speed up the process of adding new physical media to the collection.
When I bring new DVD's to my collection, I clean (physically not digitally) them log them onto my google sheets spreadsheet. Since I have logged 3000 entries on this spreadsheet before creating this automation, I have become quite fast at logging, I can log an entry in anywhere from 40 to 8 seconds if it's not too complex. More complex entries like collections can take up to 8 minutes depending on how large they are. So it is likely that the automation that will be created will be slower than my physical logging of each title, but the idea is that I could clean the DVDs while they are being logged by the system.
My idea is the phone version of the web app will be able to use the camera to scan the Barcode on the back of the DVD and will be able to get the information to fill out the spreadsheet from that barcode as it should link to not the movie, but the DVD itself.
Aim Two:
The next aim is to create a web application that I can access from anywhere that shows all of the movies and TV and titles in my collection that I own and on what formats, with all the little details on each of the titles that I select so that when I am out searching for new titles to add to my collection I don't accidentally grab one that I already have.
**Aim Three:**
The Web App also exists as a platform that I can show to others so they can decide on movie meetups what movie or movies they want me to bring to the occasion. Not that that the web app will function specifically for that use case, but the idea of having an online browser for my collection would be more helpful in that use case than describing what I have over the phone and will make it easier for them to decide from remote distances. so the web app should have comprehensive search features that can search by format, franchise, year released, genre etc.
Aim Four:
When people come over and want to watch a movie from my collection, people are often indecisive because the collection is so large and they don't know when to start. Everyone has a particular taste in movies and TV, so they might only be drawn to specific titles. This problem is multiplied when there are multiple people who want to watch a movie and everyone has a different taste. I aim to integrate into the web app some sort of search by taste filter with movie/tv taste profiles. That being, a user will enter what they like to see in titles like: after 1970, Romance, No sad endings, No action, at least 70% on Rotten Tomatoes, and that information would get saved to their taste profile. So ideally when you search and enter the taste profiles of two people who want to watch a movie, the search will find the middle ground and give them both a movie that appeals to both taste profiles and give a middle ground.
Aim Five:
Once a movie is logged in the spreadsheet, due to the size of the collection can take time to find exactly where to put it, despite being full organized. So once the movie is logged in the spreadsheet and appears in the App for the first time. The app will tell the user what two movies it should be between and what genre it comes from just to make putting it in the shelf a little easier. However, the genres listed on the spreadsheet for each movie and the genre selection that the movie actually sits in the collection can be different, so we'll probably need to add another category for "collection genre", but aside from that all titles in each genre are order alphabetically, and if there are two identical titles, they are ordered by release date to set apart remakes. Perhaps the location in collection notification could trigger after a title has been watched and is set to be returned to the collection too.
